2. Absorbed share: absorbed minority prompts ÷ minority prompts, absorbed = tail max over the last 20% of steps below τ among prompts starting at or above 10τ (analyze_v3.persistent, analyze_v3g.t17_row). At m = 1/3 the 'minority' is still classes 1 and 2.
3. T30 prediction 2: 'every class mean' is each of the three classes' mean success in the final snapshot (step 20,000), over the 120 exact runs at s = 0 (both η̃, all three m).
4. T30 prediction 4: read over every (update, η̃) cell of the linear grid at m = 1/3, the strictest reading.
5. T30 prediction 5: required at each of the two η̃ values.
6. T30 prediction 6 and R(s): R = −Δ_A/Δ_B from `check_odds_law.predict`, the first-order step-0 change of the pooled minority mean (the odds law; it applies unchanged to block features). The first-step sign is read from the exact flow at its first time point τ = 1e-3; the discrete runs' first logged step (step 100) is given as a check.
7. T30 γ_min(s): T25's definition (`check_permanence`): the smallest projection of a minority prompt's features on the majority's l2 max-margin direction (`check_late_time.l2_max_margin`), computed from the task's block features. The theory behind it (the scalar late model) was derived for the class-label toy; it is reported, not tested.
8. T31 at step 2,000: the absorbed shares use the per-prompt snapshot at step 2,000 (no tail window exists there), and 'protected and learned' at step 2,000 uses those. At the end they use the tail max.
9. T31 'first logged step at which the majority reaches 0.99': over the whole run; in the step-2,000 table only steps ≤ 2,000 count. Medians treat 'never' as infinite.
10. T31 prediction 7: a task where the pass@k arm never reaches 0.99 counts as 'later' if plain GRPO reaches it.
11. T31 prediction 1 compares the logged class means (majority and pooled minority) at all 1,001 logged steps.
12. T32 recovered: minority class mean > 0.5 at 1e14 as registered (T25's table used ≥ 0.5; no task is at 0.5). Lowest minority class mean: the smallest value on T25's 80-point grid, compared in log space.
13. T32 prediction 4: recovery time is the flow time from which the minority class mean stays above 0.5 to 1e14 (the last upward crossing), interpolated linearly in log10 time between grid points; 'earlier' is strict.
14. T32 majority class mean at 1e2, 1e3, 1e4: linear interpolation in log10 time on T25's grid.
15. T33(a) prediction 1: the 0.9 share is over all sampled pass@k runs pooled (both sources, both forms, k = 2, 4, 8). The exact part uses T31's three unscaled pass@k arms (the registered ones); the matched-step arms are reported.
16. T33(a) prediction 2: pairs whose GRPO run never reaches 0.99 are left out (their number is given). Mean pass@1 at a logged step is the logged mean success over all prompts.
17. T33(b) prediction 5: read at the switch at step 100 (the step of predictions 4, 6 and 7); step 1,000 reported.
18. T33(b) prediction 6: per-prompt success after the switch is stored only at steps 2,000 and 100,000, so pass@8 (and the conjunction) is checked at those two steps.
