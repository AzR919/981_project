## SmolLM2 diagnostic (LoRA, lr 1e-4, 200 steps)

Without truncated importance weights the reward collapses to 0.02 by step 50 and answers run to the
length limit; with truncated importance weights (C = 2) it trains normally (reward 0.81–0.84). The
earlier SmolLM2 failures were a sampler–trainer mismatch, not a property of the model.

## C7 / C8 KL (partial: 11 of 20 runs finished; 800 steps)

| Arm (finished runs) | Training success | Held-out success | Held-out declines | Held-out lost |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| no KL, G = 16 (1) | 0.99 | 0.82 | 26 | 10 |

| forward KL 0.1 (1 usable) | 0.83 | 0.75 | 3 | 0 |
| forward KL 0.5 (2) | 0.65 | 0.61–0.62 | 0, 0 | 0 |

- **Two runs broke numerically** (reverse KL 0.02 at G = 16, step 713; forward KL 0.1 seed 0, step 612):
  the KL value and the gradient norm become NaN and the model is destroyed. Cause: with β > 0 every
  sampled answer enters the loss; once the policy is far from the reference (KL of 5–8 nats per
  sequence), one sampled token's log-ratio is extreme, `exp` in the forward-KL term overflows (and a
  token the trainer scores at probability 0 gives an infinite log-ratio). `extra/llm/train.py`,
  `kl_terms`. The 8 runs still going use the same code.
- Within the finished runs, a KL penalty removes the held-out declines; at β ≥ 0.1 it also costs
  training success. The one run at reverse KL 0.02 with G = 4 keeps held-out success at the no-KL
  level with no declines: one seed, to be checked against its pair (`r2_104`, running).
